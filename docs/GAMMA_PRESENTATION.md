# AOTS⁶ — Topological Invariants Theory (Gamma-ready draft)

## Slide 1 — Title
AOTS⁶ — Topological Invariants Theory (TITSO)

## Slide 2 — Problem
How to compare complex systems beyond terminology and surface naming.

## Slide 3 — Core idea
Use invariant topological properties to detect structural convergence between architectures.

## Slide 4 — Invariants
- Origin
- Integration
- Recursivity
- Homeostasis
- Fractal structure
- Ontology
- Memory
- Emergence
- Symmetry
- Conservation
- Self-organization
- Global coherence

## Slide 5 — Matrix of Topological Collisions
Compare AOTS⁶ against external systems by structural, functional, semantic, temporal, and ontological criteria.

## Slide 6 — Invariance metrics
- Topological Invariance Index (IT)
- Topological Distance (DT)
- Collision Index (ICT)

## Slide 7 — Method
1. Define nodes.
2. Map relationships.
3. Assign weights.
4. Score each invariant.
5. Interpret the result.

## Slide 8 — Key principle
Structural similarity does not prove appropriation by itself; chronology and documentary evidence are also required.

## Slide 9 — Applications
- AI architectures
- Multi-agent systems
- Ontological frameworks
- Engineering systems
- Comparative research
